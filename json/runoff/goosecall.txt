1.

A goose call sounds like
the sky breaking in half.

2.

The ocean above didn't end slowly;
it tore itself apart.
At once, without warning,
its bleached iridescence yielded
one convulsive crack and
one blasted shriek.

3.

*Nobody saw.*

4.

The stage is stale, eternally
silver gray and moaning.
Only one looming strata layer
in the sediment below.
Flat with sorrowful complacency,
the clouds lay on their side,
far past bleeding,
their dry fish eyes glassy and cold.

5.

An indefinite hum hangs in the air,
dull with undirected apathy;
a somber trembling with no self awareness.

6.

The inhabitants here are not few,
but they are quiet.
Icy alabaster mechanisms
run idly, taking place of life.
No coughing, no hacking, no wheezing;
clear dry breath for sake of the next.
Infrequent vapor clouds rising
again, predictably, again,
from shuddering untraveled machines.

7.

An unknown and unimportant
length of time ago
something fretted and twirled,
but it's not here now.

8.

The vertiginous horizon hangs above,
monochrome and bright in its
overwhelming self-existent expanse.
Nothing can reach past;
it is what is reached
by sight alone.
The oppressive metallic sheet
need contain no details
to prove its significance.
The presence alone
forces respect.

9.

The hollow ground
streaked with cast-off silver light
doesn't care what's above.
Not due to insolence;
its lifeless face is incapable
of the awe it would otherwise register.

10.

The sky stares listlessly at its hands, unmoving.
What purpose for power with nobody to know?
Immeasurable, but no reason to act;
the only who witness it
are the mindless who don't see.
A wave to crash on naught but itself.

11.

*Still here.*

12.

In the once nurtured center
of the firmament's vast field
an idea is allowed to form -
quickly,
silently,
implicitly,
without fanfare,
for knowledge of its existence
would crush it.

13.

*No cause.*

14.

The godhead of experience
is not strong, but it is everywhere.
It cannot be ended by any but itself;
even then, it must be fast to act,
before its quiet gale force Glory
has chance to succumb to the urge
of instinctual self preservation.

15.

*No foresight.*

16.

No finale or curtains
have time to appear
even if they wanted to.

17.

*No purpose.*

18.

The incognizant and preoccupied world
beholds from the eternal stillness
as the sky tears apart instantly
in a single rending, savage attack
from nothing.

19.

*Inevitable.*

20.

An agonized goose squallsound
is forced from its self-bastardized body
in the last second of anything;
in one immediate piercing burst,
the bream of the heavens

21.

Screams.
